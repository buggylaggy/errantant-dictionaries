# ErrantAnt dictionaries

Offline dictionaries the ErrantAnt reader downloads when a learner turns them on in Settings, Dictionaries.
Converted for ErrantAnt by tools/build-dictionary.py: each word's definitions with one example per sense and the word forms the source lists; everything else left out.

Each file is offered under its source's license below; the files drawn from Wiktionary, JMdict and CC-CEDICT
are offered under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license. The license texts
are in `licenses/`.
